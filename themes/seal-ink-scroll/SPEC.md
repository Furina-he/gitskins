# Seal Ink Monograph — Visual Specification

## Aesthetic Identity
Museum-grade East Asian bookbinding and ink-wash restraint engineered for GitHub's `846px` README canvas:
- **Light Mode**: Warm Xuan paper (`#F7F3EA`) with deep charcoal ink (`#1C1917`), cinnabar vermilion seal (`#C4362C`), and celadon (`#2C5E5A`).
- **Dark Mode**: Obsidian inkstone (`#141311`) with parchment ink (`#F2ECE1`), luminous vermilion (`#E0564A`), and jade celadon (`#56A39B`).
- **Signature Geometry**: Double-hairline scroll borders, geometric corner fretwork brackets, a carved geometric vermilion square seal chop, and serene layered geometric ink-mountain paths with gentle mist drift (`prefers-reduced-motion`).
