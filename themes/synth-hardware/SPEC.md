# Tactile Industrial Synth — Visual Specification

## Aesthetic Identity
Industrial hardware synthesizer ergonomics inspired by Teenage Engineering's OP-1 Field, Dieter Rams' Braun instruments, and Elektron modular rack chassis, engineered specifically for GitHub's `846px` README canvas:
- **Light Mode**: Warm bead-blasted anodized aluminum (`#EFECE6`) with matte keycap plates (`#FAF8F4`), deep carbon silkscreen ink (`#141517`), recessed jet-black AMOLED display bays (`#111317`), and color-coded hardware controls in International Orange (`#FF5200`), Telemetry Blue (`#0088CC`), Signal Green (`#1E9E63`), and Ochre (`#D49B00`).
- **Dark Mode**: Matte Elektron charcoal chassis (`#121316`) with recessed anodized bays (`#1A1C21` / `#0D0E11`), crisp off-white silkscreen (`#F3F2EE`), and high-contrast luminous encoders & OLED traces (`#FF6B2B`, `#38BDF8`, `#34D399`, `#FACC15`).
- **Signature Geometry**: Corner countersunk hex socket screws, precision-drilled circular perforated speaker dot-grid grill, four color-coded rotary encoder knobs with 270° radial tick scales and pointer indicators, recessed AMOLED oscilloscope screen with animated synth waveform (`prefers-reduced-motion` guarded), 3.5mm TRS hex-nut patch jacks (`IN / OUT`), and tactile mechanical keycaps with LED dots.
- **No Photo Portrait (By Design)**: Pure industrial hardware instrument aesthetic — identity is anchored via custom silkscreen channel codes (`OP-DEV // FIELD SYNTH · CH-01`), display typography, OLED telemetry readout, and calibrated rotary encoders.
